Calibration — where every multiple comes from

Each category's multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the snapshot's own live-token protocols in that category. A category with fewer than 3 live comps has no median of its own, so it uses the global median across all live-token protocols instead — those rows are marked as fallback. Ratios are shown rounded to 4 decimals; each median is computed from the unrounded ratios, then rounded to 4 decimals. Back to the board
